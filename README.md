# 🌾 Farm2Market (F2M)

A **Blockchain + AI powered agricultural marketplace** that connects farmers directly with buyers while ensuring transparency, fair pricing, and traceability in the agricultural supply chain.

Farm2Market integrates **blockchain smart contracts, AI price prediction, and QR-based traceability** to reduce intermediaries and empower farmers with data-driven market insights.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🌱 Direct Farmer-to-Buyer Marketplace
- Farmers can list agricultural produce
- Buyers can browse products and place orders
- Eliminates unnecessary middlemen

### 🔗 Blockchain-based Transactions
- Smart contracts ensure **secure and trustless payments**
- Escrow mechanism protects both buyers and farmers
- Immutable transaction records

### 📦 QR Code Traceability
- Every produce shipment is linked to a **QR code**
- Buyers can verify origin and product journey
- Ensures transparency across the supply chain

### 🤖 AI Price Prediction
- Predicts crop prices using **historical agricultural data**
- Helps farmers decide optimal selling prices
- Reduces market volatility risks

### 📱 Mobile/Web Interface
Farmers can:
- List produce
- Manage orders
- View predicted prices

Buyers can:
- Browse marketplace
- Verify product provenance
- Purchase directly from farmers
